SkeleKit uses a two-pass measure and arrange engine over native UIKit views. Its primary layout containers are:

- `Grid` for row-and-column layouts with auto, pixel and star sizing
- `StackPanel` for vertical or horizontal flows
- `Overlay` for layered content
- `Border` for padding, backgrounds, strokes, corners and shadows
- `ScrollView` for safe-area-aware scrolling

Detailed layout examples are coming next. The [Gallery](https://github.com/IcySnex/SkeleKit/tree/main/Source/Samples/SkeleKit.Gallery) provides runnable examples in the meantime.
